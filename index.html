03 // Chapter Pod & Prism Governance

Five roles. One pause rule.

Grounded directly in Aurora's Founding Charter: one student can hold two roles in a 3-person launch pod, expanding cleanly to five dedicated owners as retainers grow.

Editorial Lead

Role 02 // Care & BD Logistics

Producer

Owns schedules, 24h guest locks, 18+ written permission records, factual preview sign-offs, and warm client expansion.

Role 03 // Image & Sound

Production Lead

Owns eye-level natural framing, crisp wireless lav audio, lighting consistency, and chapter kit care.

Role 04 // Craft & Turnaround

Editor

Owns delivery quality, honest pacing without distorting meaning, crisp captions, 7-day ship, and finals backup training.

Role 05 // Reach & Moderation

Publishing Lead

Owns participant credits, cross-channel distribution, active harassment moderation, and client attribution links.

Universal Release Pause Rule & The Customer Journey Council

Quality compounds across semesters

Any chapter member may pause a release for a concrete accuracy, permission, or participant-care issue until resolved and logged by the Producer. Prism admission stays governed independently by The Customer Journey Council through research, peer review, and a veto gate -- never sold to sponsors. Every seat pairs with a paid Shadow Semester understudy before graduation.
